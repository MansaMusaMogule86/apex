RIT Dubai Co-op Knowledge Base

Co-op Definition
The co-op program is a structured, work-integrated learning experience where students apply academic knowledge in a real workplace as part of degree progression.

Eligibility
Students must have a CGPA greater than or equal to 2.0 to be eligible for co-op registration.

Co-op Durations
Engineering: 48 weeks
Computing: 24 weeks
Business: 16 weeks
Psychology: 10 weeks

Rules
1. Students must register for co-op one semester before the intended co-op start.
2. Students must submit the signed contract within 5 days of receiving or signing it.
3. Late registration may be rejected based on policy and timeline constraints.
